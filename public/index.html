HOW TO PLAY RIFT POSSESSION

Master the 37-hex grid with strategic champion knowledge

1

Objective & The Hex Grid

The board consists of 37 hexagonal tiles arranged in a radius-3 grid. Each hex has a specific category criterion (Region, Role, Skin Set, Mechanic, Species, Release Year, Resource, Range, or Gender). Your goal is to capture more tiles than your rival!

2

How a Turn Works

  • Select a Hex: Click an eligible tile on the grid.
  • Name a Valid Champion: Type any League champion that satisfies that tile's requirement (e.g., Ionia, True Damage, Spirit Blossom, Yordle) and hit Enter or submit.
  • Hex Claimed: The tile is painted in your team's color with the champion's portrait!
3

Special Match Rules (Toggles)

HEX CHAINING

When ON, after your opening turn you must claim a hex that is adjacent (touching) to your existing claimed territory. When OFF, you can pick any tile on the entire board.

CHAMPION LOCKING

When ON, once a champion is successfully used anywhere, it is locked and cannot be used again by either player for the rest of the game.

4
